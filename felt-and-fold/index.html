About the coach

After each hand you played, the coach looks at every decision you made. It compares your action with a baseline strategy. Each decision gets a grade and a number: the big blinds you gave up compared with that baseline.

Where the baseline comes from

Before the flop, the baseline is a set of opening, 3-bet and defence charts. Stacks of 15bb or less use push/fold charts: go all in or fold.

After the flop, the baseline is usually an estimate. It weighs your chance to win against the hands your opponent likely holds, the pot odds and the board. It is a good guide, not a solver.

Pots with two players can get a real solver answer. Some flops come from a library of solved spots. On a computer, turn and river spots are solved live while you read the review. Pots with three or more players always use the estimate.

The labels

Solved
Solved for this exact spot, from the start of the street, with ranges from the hand so far.
Library
From a solved flop like this one. Suits are mapped onto your board, and bet sizes are scaled to your pot.
Estimate
A rough estimate. Treat its grade as advice. It never goes past Mistake unless the spot is clear-cut.
Chart
From the preflop chart for this position and stack depth.
Nash
From the push/fold chart for stacks of 15bb or less.

Grades

Good Inaccuracy Mistake Blunder

Each decision shows the baseline's mix of plays as a bar. The bar's colours are actions, not grades: check, call, bet or raise (lighter for smaller sizes), all-in and fold. The marker shows your play. An action the baseline takes at least 15% of the time counts as Good, if your size is close.

Each grade comes with a reason. Tap an underlined term in it to see what the term means.

The players

Opponents come in five tiers, from Beginner to Solver, and seven styles. Only Sharks adjust to how you play. Their names are made up.

When your read says a player is easy to beat, such as a beginner or a calling station, a heads-up decision after the flop is graded against how that kind of player plays. The review then adds a “vs name” line with the plan.

Live solving is skipped on phones. Your settings, bankroll and records are saved in this browser only.
